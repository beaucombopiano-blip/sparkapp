“Perfection is the enemy of progress.”

Every feature in SPARK follows this idea. You don't need a perfect plan to start, and you don't need a perfect week to keep going.

The problem

Your feed only shows the finish line.

Social media is full of "how I built a startup in a month." Nobody posts week three, when nothing is working. So when your own project gets hard, it feels like something is wrong with you or your idea.

Usually, nothing is wrong. You're in the normal middle of the process. SPARK makes that middle visible, gives you a plan through it, and connects you with people who've been there.

How it works

Everything you need to get from idea to done.

Tasks & milestones

Break your project into steps, mark the milestones, and watch your progress bar fill as you check things off.

Journal

Your journey in photos. Save drafts, setbacks, and wins so you can see how far you've come, even before results show up.

Schedule

Set your work sessions around school and life. SPARK builds a realistic timeline that fits the time you actually have.

Resources & help

Ask anything about your project and get guidance, resources, and connections to mentors who've built something similar.
